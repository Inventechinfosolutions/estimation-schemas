### 01. Person, Post, Role & Access Governance
- **Type:** Shared
- **Summary:** Staff identity as Person mapped to unit Posts (Depot, Workshop, Stores, Central Office) with Roles and 2FA.
- **Source:** Derived from scope and functional areas in the provided documents.
- **In Scope:** Core capabilities and workflows described for the module.
- **Out of Scope:** Barcode/scanner hardware purchase, SMS usage charges, classroom training delivery, and unrelated BMTC IT products unless separately ordered.
- **Key Actors:** Authorised BMTC staff Posts at Depot, Zone, Workshop, Stores and Central Office.
- **Dependencies:** Module 01 Person–Post–Role; location masters; reporting readiness depends on Module 14.
- **Notes:** Designed to be estimable as a cohesive delivery unit.

***Excluded / Embedded Capabilities***

- Workflow and document handling are embedded in operational features; there is no standalone DMS module.
- Hosting (MeitY-empaneled, bidder-provided) and 12-month AMC after Go-Live are in the bid (Addendum-1231 and pre-bid replies).
- Scanner/hardware purchase, SMS usage charges and classroom training delivery remain outside feature hours unless separately ordered.

***Assumptions***

- Module and feature IDs originate in the Step 1 SOW to preserve alignment with downstream extraction.
- Access is Person → Post → Role, scoped by Depot / Zone / Workshop / Stores / Central Office.
- AMC duration is 12 months from Go-Live (Addendum-1231); optional further year at same price remains as original contract-period clause unless BMTC restates it.

***Risks***

- Unclear supervising Posts would misroute job-card, stores and purchase approvals.
- AVLS field mapping and legacy IT extracts not confirmed early would delay KM, fuel and migration.

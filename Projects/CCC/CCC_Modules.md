***Module Details***

### 01. Person, Post, Role and Access Governance
- **Type:** Shared
- **Summary:** Staff identity as Person mapped to FCS Posts with Roles for CCC, district and godown-scoped access.
- **Source:** Derived from CCC Tender software solution scope and confirmed estimation boundaries.
- **In Scope:** Application software capabilities listed in the SOW key capabilities.
- **Out of Scope:** CCC hardware, GPS/CCTV field devices, KSDC INR, AMC/O&M manpower.
- **Key Actors:** FCS staff Posts at State CCC, District, Godown and Vigilance as mapped in Module 01.
- **Dependencies:** Module 01 Person–Post–Role; integrations depend on Module 12.
- **Notes:** Estimable as a cohesive delivery unit.

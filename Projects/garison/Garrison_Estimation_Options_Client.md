# Garrison Website and Moulding Catalogue: Estimate Options

**Client:** Garrison Industries  
**Date:** 7 October 2026

This document sets out three options for Garrison's new website and moulding catalogue. Each option includes a new website design. The options differ in how the product catalogue is set up and how it is maintained after the website goes live.

## 2. Options Matrix

All three options include the same new website. The main difference is who looks after the product catalogue once the website is live.

- **Options 1 and 2:** We set up the products once. Any later changes are made by our developer.
- **Option 3:** Garrison gets its own easy-to-use control panel, so its staff can add, edit, review and publish products themselves. The product pages also carry much more detail.

## 3. Important Notes

1. All hours are our team's effort. They assume about 350 moulding products and about 15 pages on the current website. Additional products or pages will be charged at the same rate.
2. None of the options include project management, hosting or domain charges, other third-party fees, new written content or photography.
3. Garrison will need written permission from Brenlo to use Brenlo's product information. Options 1 and 3 also need permission to use Brenlo's pictures.
4. Garrison will need to give us access to its domain settings so the new website can go live. Company email will not be affected.
5. In Option 2, the AI picture service is charged at actual cost and will not exceed the limit shown in the summary.
6. These are indicative estimates. We will confirm them once the final number of products and pages is known.

# Garrison Website & Catalogue — Estimation Options

**Client:** Garrison Industries  
**Date:** 2026-10-07

Garrison has advised that products change rarely, so catalogue set-up is a one-time activity. Options 1 and 2 therefore have no administration portal; Option 3 is the full platform with self-service administration.

## What each option delivers

- **Option 1 — Website design and product migration.** New website design and build (home, company pages, contact form, catalogue pages with filters and search), one-time migration of moulding products with their details and images from the Brenlo catalogue, SEO, hosting and launch. No admin portal.
- **Option 2 — Option 1 plus AI product images.** As Option 1, but product images are original AI-assisted renders built from dimension-accurate outlines instead of copied photographs, plus AI lifestyle and banner images. AI service fees are pass-through.
- **Option 3 — Premium online catalogue platform.** Admin portal with roles, configurable categories and attributes, publishing workflow, content rights tracking, bulk import and advanced search. Excludes the UX/UI design phase and project management.

## Legal note

All options use Brenlo's product data; Garrison should hold Brenlo's written permission. Option 2 avoids publishing Brenlo photographs but the profiles shown remain Brenlo designs, and AI images carry their own considerations (accuracy, ownership, provider terms). Garrison's counsel should review before go-live. This is not legal advice.

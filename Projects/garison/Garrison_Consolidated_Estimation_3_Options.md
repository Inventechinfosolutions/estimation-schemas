# Garrison Website & Moulding Catalogue — Consolidated Estimate

**Client:** Garrison Industries  
**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Effort unit:** person-hours

## 1. Introduction

This document brings together three options for Garrison Industries' website and moulding catalogue. Garrison has advised that products change rarely, so Options 1 and 2 set up the catalogue once and have no administration portal. Option 3 is the full platform with self-service administration.

- **Option 1 — Website design and product migration:** new website design and build, with the Brenlo moulding products and their details migrated once.
- **Option 2 — Option 1 plus AI product images:** as Option 1, with original AI-assisted product images and lifestyle imagery instead of copied photographs.
- **Option 3 — Premium online catalogue platform:** the same new website design and company pages as Options 1 and 2, plus admin portal, configurable catalogue, publishing workflow, rights tracking, bulk import and advanced search.

### Why each picture needs work

An AI tool cannot draw a correct moulding just from its name. Asked for a '5¼-inch colonial crown', it draws a crown that looks right but has the wrong shape. Because visitors choose mouldings by their exact shape, every picture must be accurate. For each product we:

1. Draw the real outline of the moulding at its real size.
2. Turn the outline into a plain 3D picture, so the shape is exactly right.
3. Let the AI add a realistic wood or paint finish and lighting.
4. Check the picture against the outline before it goes on the website.

Steps 1 and 4 are repeated for all 350 products (140 hours). The remaining 185 hours set up the process once. AI service fees are separate (see the summary table).

### Ways to reduce the cost of Option 2

| Choice | Saving | What changes |
|---|---|---|

| Let the AI create pictures without the outline and 3D steps | About 150 hours | Pictures will often show the wrong shape, which is risky for a product catalogue |
| Use AI only for room, banner and category pictures; product pages show a clean outline drawing | Roughly 250–300 hours (quoted on request) | Product pictures look technical rather than like photographs |

## 7. Which Option Is Right for Garrison

- **Option 1** is right if the product range will stay small and rarely change, Garrison is happy to ask a developer for the occasional change, and it wants the lowest cost and the quickest launch. Brenlo's permission to use its pictures is needed.
- **Option 2** is right in the same situation if Garrison prefers its own new pictures instead of using Brenlo's.
- **Option 3** is right if Garrison plans to sell more types of products (for example doors, sheet goods or products from other suppliers), several staff will look after the catalogue, it wants drawings and PDF sheets on product pages, it wants changes approved before they go live, or it does not want to rely on a developer for product changes.

Option 3 is an investment in a catalogue that Garrison runs and grows itself. If the range will stay as it is, Option 1 or 2 is the better value. Garrison can also start with Option 1 or 2 and move to Option 3 later: the product information we prepare can be loaded into Option 3, so that work is not wasted.

## Overview

The IMA Online Claim Settlement System is designed to digitize the complete claim settlement process carried out by the Competent Authority under the Karnataka Protection of Interest of Depositors in Financial Establishments (KPID) Act.

The process begins only after a Financial Establishment (FE) is declared fraudulent and the responsibility of settling investor claims is handed over to the Competent Authority.

---

## Phases

### Phase 1 – Fraud Happens

A Financial Establishment (FE) collects money from the public through multiple investment schemes.

Examples include:

- IMA Jewels  
- Lancer Finance  
- Innovative Business Centre  
- Any other entity notified under the KPID Act  

Thousands of investors invest in various schemes.

A single investor may:

- Invest in multiple schemes.  
- Make multiple investments under the same scheme.  
- Invest in different branches.  
- Invest in multiple Fraudulent Entities.  

### Phase 2 – Fraud is Identified

The FE fails to repay depositors.

Typical situations include:

- Maturity amount not paid  
- Interest payments stopped  
- Deposits not returned  
- Company offices closed  
- Directors become unavailable  

Investors submit complaints to the Police and Government authorities.

### Phase 3 – Criminal Investigation

The Police and Investigation Agencies conduct the investigation.

Activities include:

- Registering the FIR  
- Collecting evidence  
- Identifying Directors and Promoters  
- Seizing company records  
- Freezing bank accounts  
- Identifying company assets  
- Filing the Charge Sheet before the Court  

This process is outside the scope of the proposed application. However, investigation references such as FIR Number, Court Case Number, and Government Notification are maintained for reference.

**Another Route:** (i) Complaint (ii) Suo-moto action on advertisement (iii) Inputs from RBI/SLCC/Ombudsman (iv) Direct information to DC/AC/Fraud cell of DC Office/SPLOCA/Regional commissioner, Bengaluru (Secretary Economic Offences).

### Phase 4 – Government Invokes KPID Act

The Government issues 3(2) on the company property as a Fraudulent Entity (FE).

The Government:

- Invokes the KPID Act.  
- Appoints the Competent Authority.  
- Issues Ad-interim attachment of the FE’s assets.  
  - a) Issue of preliminary orders of investigation  
  - b) “Market Intelligence”  
  - c) Notices to suspected fraudster to provide replies/documents  
  - d) Freezing orders under rule 5  
  - e) Report to Government  

From this stage onwards, the **online system is not acceptable** due to age profile & social economic profile of the claimants and the necessity of original docs for verification, as required under BSA, 2023. Hence, online will only be allowed for out of Karnataka claimants.

## Modules

### Module 1 – Fraudulent Entity (FE) Management / Database Management

#### Objective

Create and maintain complete details of every Fraudulent Entity in separate interlinked folders with unique ID, key elements & key words linked, so that at Specific button commands/queries raw data/analytical info can be extracted, reported & compared and certain decision reports projected on dashboards on Web Application already in place.

Data of FIR, Charge Sheets, Accused, Complaint, KPIDFE Court proceedings shall be scanned and transferred to word/excel and edited as required, for analytical purposes.

Cross Verification of claims with the seized database of the FE should be covered.

Processes relating to ineligible depositors, deceased depositors, legal heirs, joint deposits, third party payments, lost receipts and cash investments to be covered.

Penny-drop verification, excess-payment analysis and audit trail to be included.

The system should prepare the equitable distribution statement and the report required for filing before the Special Court, on being fed relevant data into an input format.

**a)** Data of seizures/ad-interim attachments for post Seizure data and verification including recording of relevant documents after translation & linking them through Key Words (Eg. Mahazar/Financial Establishment/Survey number etc., & unique ID).  
**b)** Analysis of seized papers, database, translation etc.,  
**c)** Market intelligence elements.  
**d)** Social media & Media information analysis and concise reporting along with document storage linked to key words & unique ID.

#### Activities

- Register FE details.  
- Capture Directors and Promoters.  
- Configure Investment Schemes.  
- Store Court Case information.  
- Store FIR references.  
- Upload Government Notifications.  
- Upload Court Orders.  
- Maintain Company Bank Accounts.  
- Maintain Claimant Database.  
- Maintain current case status.  

---

### Module 2 – Asset Attachment & Recovery Management

#### Objective

Maintain all Court-attached assets and recovered funds. Update through an input format.

#### Activities

- Register attached assets & Transfer title, create E-Khata, obtain and pay dues till Khata is created, before auction.  
- Upload attachment orders.  
- Record valuation details in E-Format based on valuation manual.  
- Track auction process in consultation with E-Governance.  

#### Output

Recovered amount available for settlement.

---

### Module 3 – Claim & Depositor Master & Claims Management

#### Objective

**(i)** Direct recording of soft data from hard copy in specified format using searchable (a) OCR (b) Translation (c) Conversion of format (eg. JPEG to PDF to DOC).  

**(ii)** Create a unique profile with 4 parameters of matching for every depositor, & query friendly database with key word, address identification, (tallying of Pin Code to actual areas & verifying address, tallying addresses & Name details given in Aadhaar/PAN/EPIC with Bank Pass Book, Claim Forms, and auto generate mismatch queries.

#### Activities

**Capture:**

- Personal Information  
- Aadhaar  
- PAN  
- Contact Details  
- Bank Accounts  
- Nominee Details  
- Legal Heir Details  

#### Output

A single depositor profile is maintained across all Fraudulent Entities.

---

### Module 4 – Investment Portfolio Management

The module performs comparisons across different FE databases with FIR and Seizures Asset databases. This involves using single and multiple separate and linked parameters to identify duplication, collusion, and other legal requirements.

Key features include:

- **Auto-calculation & Analytics:** Covers deposits, interest received, maturity dates, and violations of those dates. It calculates promised interest versus actual interest paid.  
- **Financial Tracking:** Determines the outstanding balance by treating interest paid as principal repaid.  
- **Categorization:** Identification of various roles such as types of depositors, colluders, or the accused based on data analytics.  
- **Verification:** Analysis of Bank returns (after scan-based transfer), analysis of data, and cross-verification with claims data.  
- **Automation:** Recording Bank Officer email addresses to enable auto-verification, unless the data is submitted directly by Bank Officers.  

#### Objective

Maintain every investment made by a depositor.

#### Activities

**Capture:**

- Fraudulent Entity  
- Investment Scheme  
- Customer ID  
- Bond / MOU Number  
- Deposit Receipt  
- Investment Date  
- Maturity Date  
- Deposit Amount  
- Interest Promised  
- Amount Already Received  
- Outstanding Balance  

#### Output

Complete investment history available.

---

### 3A – Claims (Claim Notification)

#### Objective

Invite eligible investors to submit claims.

#### Activities

Create a claim notification containing:

- Eligible FE (Fraudulent Entity)  
- Eligible Schemes  
- Claim Submission Period  
- Eligibility Criteria  
- Required Documents  
- Instructions  

Publish the notification through the portal.

#### Output

The claim window becomes active.

---

### Module 3 B – Online Claim Submission

**Note (as printed):** Not required as an online limited access system exists.

#### Objective

Enable investors to submit claims online.

#### Activities

- The Investor logs into the portal.  
- The Investor selects:  
  - Fraudulent Entity  
  - Investment Scheme  
  - Investment Record  
- The Investor confirms:  
  - Personal Details  
  - Investment Details  
  - Refund Bank Account  
- The Investor uploads:  
  - Identity Proof  
  - Investment Proof  
  - Bank Documents  
  - Nominee / Legal Heir Documents (if applicable)  
  - Affidavit  
  - Statement of Investment  

System validates mandatory information before submission.

#### Output

Claim submitted successfully.

---

### 3C – Claim Verification & Evaluation

#### Objective

Verify the submitted claim and determine eligibility.

#### Activities

IMA Officer verifies:

- Identity  
- Investment Details  
- Scheme  
- Customer ID  
- Deposit Records  
- Uploaded Documents  
- Bank Details  
- Previous Returns Received  
- Existing Claims  
- Eligibility  

If required, the officer requests additional documents through the portal.  
The claimant uploads additional documents.  
The officer continues the evaluation.

#### Output

Verified claim ready for approval.

---

### Claim Approval

#### Objective

Approve eligible claims.

#### Activities

Competent Authority reviews the verification report.

Decision may be:

- Approved  
- Partially Approved  
- Rejected  
- Pending Clarification  

Remarks are recorded.

#### Output

Claim decision finalized.

---

### Module 4A – Recovery & Settlement Management

#### Objective

Calculate settlement based on recovered funds.

#### Activities

**Record:**

- Total Recovered Amount  
- Total Approved Liability  
- Available Settlement Fund  

**System calculates:**

- Eligible Amount  
- Previous Returns Received  
- Previous Settlements  
- Net Payable Amount  

#### Output

Settlement amount finalised.

---

### Module 4B – Payment & Claim Closure

#### Objective

Release payment and close claim.

#### Activities

- Generate payment batches.  
- Transfer payment through:  
  - NEFT  
  - RTGS  
- **Record:**  
  - Payment Reference  
  - Transaction Number  
  - Payment Status  
- **Update:**  
  - Claim Status  
  - Investment Balance  
  - Payment History  

#### Output

Claim successfully settled and closed.

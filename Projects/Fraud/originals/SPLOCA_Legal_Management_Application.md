## 2. Search Facility

Keyword search allowing both natural language search (Google-like search; e.g. "What are the elements of battery?") and Boolean terms and connectors search (e.g. Football /p Baseball searches for documents in which "Football" and "Baseball" appear in the same paragraph).

Search box to enter keywords.

**A. Natural Language Search**

"Search Within Results" using key words

**B. Boolean Terms and Connector**

- AND, OR, NOT,
- /p (same paragraph),
- /s (same sentence),
- /pg (same page),
- /d (same document)
- "" (quotation mark) for a phrase
- /# (where # is a number)
- ! (Root expander - to retrieve words with variant endings. For example, Obey! retrieves Obey, Obeys, Obeyed, Obeying)
- () parenthesis to group terms

**C.** Highlight & hyperlink keywords with document address in Results

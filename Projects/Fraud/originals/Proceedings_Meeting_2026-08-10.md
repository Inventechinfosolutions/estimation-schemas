## Discussion points / requirements

1. The proposed solution must be a dedicated, role-based application compatible with the existing legacy system. It should include secure web and Android components specifically for official work. It is emphasized that this should not be merely a public-facing portal (which already exists).

2. The claimant identity verification process (including phone numbers and addresses) must be reliable, accurate, and error-free. The system is required to provide accurate extraction and conversion of the contents of every uploaded PDF and JPEG file into a searchable format (OCR functionality) and editable text, without altering the source content. The claimant documents like photos/JPEG shall be screened through a suitable Python application to check for forgery or alterations. The online verification of Aadhaar is existing; PAN to be provided.

3. Phone number shall be verified by OTP. Address shall be verified by Pin code/Digi pin and geographical location comparison from database. If discrepancy arises it will be reverified by RPAD.

4. The claimant's deposit and refund bank accounts shall be verified automatically with reference to the account-holder name, account number, branch name, IFSC code and other relevant bank particulars. Comparison of name to recorded claim application or ID document, comparison of account to no of digits to be confirmed from bank later physically and comparison of Bank, Branches in Karnataka and IFSC codes in Karnataka, shall be enabled from database. Auto generation of notices from templates as per notified forms shall be provided.

5. The system shall include a mechanism for conducting and recording penny-drop verification conducted by the contracted bank together with the verification of results and audit trail of payments.

6. A Legal Web Application and an Integrated Legal Task Management module with Android application shall be developed for systematic management of court matters, including case particulars, filings, hearing dates, court attendance, court orders, compliance status and the next action required.

7. The integrated Task Management module shall assign and track matter-related and other official tasks. A Court Attendance and Expense Management component shall record attendance, authorized expenses and their linkage to the concerned court matter. A legal digital library of acts, rules, key orders, special court orders and templates of various legal documents: 5(2) petition, various notices as per rules, 7(1) report, 8(1) report, SOB, PWR, Caveat, IA, SLP etc. in font and spacing approved by courts where master data, static data and dynamic data and templates required by these modules shall be centrally maintained.

8. Secure real-time channels may be proposed for internal team collaboration, group meetings, document sharing, audio visual sharing and for authorized coordination between the operational units and the Legal cell. Static or master data required by these modules shall be centrally maintained.

9. A Seized Property Valuation and Asset Management module with Android application shall be developed for seized and attached properties. Beginning with 3(2) and culminating in auction and deposit to approved. Authorized office users shall be able to create the asset record, 3(2) prepared code and categorize and link to location, address and photo and allow to register approved valuers, assign assets for valuation, update records of rights and physical status, and search properties by Financial Establishment name, address and other relevant keywords. For moveable properties the location, model, likely date of purchase, likely price, etc. shall be recorded and the bank, account and other details shall also be recorded. For cash and foreign currency after checking for quantity, value and damage during handover, application to replace damaged notes to RBI shall be automated in the appropriate format.

10. The Valuation Android Application shall display to the assigned valuer the basic asset particulars and documents already entered or uploaded by the office. It shall permit the valuer to retrieve assigned data from the web application, obtain information from approved secure sources, and enter the remaining valuation particulars through method-specific data-entry or API fields.

11. The Valuation Android Application shall capture photographs with GIS or geotagging, videos and the valuer's real-time location, together with the system-generated date and time, during field inspection and submission of valuation data.

12. The valuation module shall generate reports for the Competent Authority from the data entered by the valuer and facilitate assessment of the valuation report on key parameters, including time taken, asset category, valuation method adopted, adjustment factors used, the relevant statutory reference, including Section 7(1) where applicable, and guidance value. The same module shall also allow a digital layout/CAD drawing of the property made by competent officers to be uploaded and utilized for maintenance and security.

13. The web application shall enable authorized administrators to create, update, deactivate and manage officers, other contract staff, security guards and valuer user credentials and related asset or valuer master data. The valuation module shall also prepare the valuer's bill and voucher from the approved particulars. A sub-module to be used by admin and IT shall be the property and inventory module which shall have complete, unique coded inventory with location and raising of maintenance tickets by users for IT hardware, IT software, Application web apps, other machinery and equipment.

14. The system shall provide for follow-up of depositor complaints and public grievances, including tracking of the complaint, FIR, investigation and charge-sheet stages and the action taken at each stage.

15. As market intelligence is collected and handled confidentially and investigation procedures are confidential, the market-intelligence process and data and investigation process shall be excluded from the proposed application, except to the extent of Business Process Flow and the proposed application, except to the extent of issue of various notices from form templates.

16. In the entire package generative AI shall not be used nor accepted. Any AI-assisted functionality shall be confined to build and deploy agent tools / approved rule-based or agentic automation, subject to human verification and a complete audit trail.

17. The system shall provide a modifiable, searchable flag or report to identify, across different Financial Establishments, depositors who have filed applications under the Right to Information Act, 2005 seeking third-party information.

18. A common database architecture shall interconnect the claims, legal, matter-management, task-management, court-attendance and expense, collaboration, static-data, property-management and valuation modules. Authorized users shall be able to access linked information without duplicate data entry, subject to role-based access controls.

19. The solution shall include the required cloud architecture and deployment of all approved services on the existing designated server. Necessary arrangements for secure Android distribution and licensing, including Play Store licensing where specifically approved, shall have to be provided. Licensing shall not be speculative but clear. For all in-house applications and codes and software, the intellectual property rights, source codes etc. shall solely lie with SPLOCA and no use / re-use or commercial or advertisement or research related activity shall be permitted without payment of fees of the SPLOCA, based on the value of the product.

20. Actual claimant-level equitable-distribution data shall not be shared for development or testing. Only anonymized or synthetic data may be used for those purposes.

21. The system shall be capable of generating draft letters, reports, notices, freezing orders, warrants, etc. to the concerned Tahsildar, Banks, Claimants or other competent authority or Government requesting compliance through stored templates and interfaces.

22. The system shall automatically generate a unique code for each Financial Establishment, comprising the FE number, FE type and the tri-lateral index assigned to that Financial Establishment, and for each seized property with Google location.

23. Each module and sub-module shall be provided with in-built access control, security, data backup, training and FAQ modules.

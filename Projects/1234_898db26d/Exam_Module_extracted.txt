1. Introduction
The proposed Examination Module will provide an end-to-end digital platform for candidate registration, examination scheduling, hall ticket generation, secure examination conduct, evaluation, result publication, certificate generation, and certificate verification.
The system will support two categories of candidates:
Government Department Employees 
Employees of PSUs, Boards, Corporations, Societies and other eligible organisations 
The system will provide controlled access to examinations and ensure that candidates can take the examination only from authorised examination centres configured and approved by the department.
2. Objectives
The key objectives of the Examination Module are:
Provide secure candidate login using OTP. 
Enable online examination registration. 
Integrate with KGID for Government Department Employees. 
Automate candidate information retrieval through KGID integration. 
Support examination fee management. 
Allow candidates to select preferred examination centres. 
Facilitate examination scheduling and centre allocation. 
Generate and provide hall tickets digitally. 
Conduct secure computer-based examinations. 
Generate multiple question paper versions through randomisation. 
Ensure examination access only from authorised examination centres. 
Record examination activity for audit and monitoring. 
Generate results immediately after submission, wherever applicable. 
Generate digitally verifiable certificates with QR codes. 
Allow third parties to verify certificates through QR code.

Candidate enters OTP.

Resend OTP control.

18. Authorised Examination Centre Restriction
Critical Requirement
The examination shall NOT be accessible from arbitrary locations or from the candidate's personal computer/home system.
The examination can be conducted only from an authorised examination centre configured and approved in the system.
This requirement shall be implemented as a core security and examination-control feature.
18.1 Centre-Based Examination Access
Each authorised examination centre shall be configured in the system.
The centre shall have:
Unique Centre ID 
Centre Name 
Address 
Approved capacity 
Centre administrator/invigilator user 
Approved examination sessions 
Authorised systems/devices/network configuration, as applicable 
18.2 Candidate-Centre Mapping
A candidate shall be mapped to a specific examination centre and examination session.
For example:
Candidate: ABCExamination: Departmental ExaminationDate: 15 October 2026Time: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PMCentre: Centre 01Seat/Computer: System 25
The candidate shall be allowed to take the examination only against this authorised examination session.
19. Technical Enforcement of Authorised Centre
To prevent candidates from accessing the examination remotely, the system shall implement one or more centre-level access controls based on the final technical infrastructure approved by the client.
Recommended approach
The examination environment should use:
Authorised Centre + Authorised Network + Authorised Device + Candidate Allocation + Examination Session
The examination system shall validate:
Whether the candidate is scheduled for the examination. 
Whether the examination session is currently active. 
Whether the candidate is mapped to the centre. 
Whether the request originates from the authorised centre network/device environment. 
Whether the candidate is permitted to start the examination. 
Whether the candidate has already attempted/completed the examination. 
If any validation fails, the system shall prevent examination access.
Example
If a candidate tries to access the examination from:
Home laptop – Access Denied 
Mobile phone – Access Denied 
Another city/network – Access Denied 
Unauthorised computer at the centre – Access Denied 
Authorised computer at the allotted centre during the scheduled session – Access Allowed

45. Authorised Examination Centre Access Requirement
The examination shall be conducted only at examination centres that are authorised and configured in the system by the Department. Candidates shall not be permitted to access or attempt the examination from their home, personal device, unauthorised location, unauthorised network, or any centre other than the centre allotted to them.
The system shall ensure that a candidate can start the examination only when all the following conditions are satisfied:
The candidate is registered and eligible for the examination. 
The candidate is allotted to the particular examination centre. 
The candidate is scheduled for the specific examination date and time. 
The candidate is accessing the examination through an authorised system/device available at the allotted examination centre. 
The examination centre and examination session are active and authorised in the system. 
The candidate's examination session has been validated/activated by the authorised centre or invigilator. 
The system shall validate the examination centre and session before granting access to the examination. If the candidate attempts to access the examination from an unauthorised location, device, network, centre, or outside the scheduled examination session, the system shall deny access.
The examination centre shall be managed through the administration module, where authorised centre details, centre capacity, examination schedules, candidate allocation, and centre users can be configured.
The final technical mechanism for enforcing centre-only access, such as authorised network/IP validation, device whitelisting, secure examination environment, centre-level authentication, or a combination of these controls, shall be finalised based on the Department's infrastructure and security requirements.
